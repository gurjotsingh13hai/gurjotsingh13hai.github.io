SD-SI

A private, internal tool for one immigration practice in Canada. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

SD-SI helps the practice's own staff prepare immigration application packages. It works only inside the practice's own Google account:

  • it reads the folders the staff have prepared in that account's Google Drive;
  • it saves the finished package back into the same folder;
  • it writes a short run log to a Google Sheet in the same Drive;
  • it sends short status emails from that account to the practice's own staff.

A person at the practice reviews every package. SD-SI never submits anything to any government portal.

Who signs in

Only the practice's own Google account signs in to SD-SI. No one else is asked for access.
